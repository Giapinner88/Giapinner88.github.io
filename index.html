About

My research focuses on the dynamics, estimation, and control of underactuated and flexible robotic systems. I am interested in reduced-order models, such as pseudo-rigid-body (PRB) chains, that stay accurate under large deformation yet are light enough to run inside a real-time control loop, and in pairing them with nonlinear and sampling-based model predictive control and with state estimation. A recurring theme is control under minimal sensing: the deformation of a flexible body is often not measured, so it must be estimated, and model mismatch can bias both the estimate and the controller acting on it. I also study learning-based predictors of dynamic behavior from sparse observations, with the aim of exploiting a flexible body's passive dynamics for tasks such as swing-up, tool manipulation, and locomotion.

Research

Minimal-sensing phantom bending

HardwareJournal in preparation

A cart-pole with a flexible TPU beam sensed only at the cart and the base. Reduced-order observers can invent a bending that is not there (phantom bending). A dual-channel UKF and a real-time NMPC balance the beam in simulation and on the physical rig.

UKF · NMPC · PRB · Real-time control

Dynamic tool manipulation

Simulation + learningICRA 2027, under review

A learned model predicts a flexible rod's endpoint response from sparse visual markers, and sampling-based MPC uses it to bring the tool tip beyond its quasi-static reach.

Learned dynamics · Information-theoretic MPC · MuJoCo

Global swing-up via NMPC

SimulationHUST Student Research Conference

A two-segment PRB model and a jaxopt NMPC that exploits the whip effect to swing up an underactuated flexible inverted pendulum at low solve latency.

NMPC · PRB · JAX / jaxopt

High-fidelity PRB modeling

SimulationICEMA 2025

A floating-frame pseudo-rigid-body model with closed-form joint stiffness identification that matches continuum references closely at high resolution.

Lagrangian dynamics · Large deflection · Parameter identification

Vibration locomotion of a Flix-Walker

SimulationIFToMM ISRM 2026

Behavior maps of a vibration-driven walker in MuJoCo, and a posture-alignment navigation strategy that reaches every benchmark goal under payload and friction changes.

Contact dynamics · Motion primitives · MuJoCo
